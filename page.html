About

Lorian Hans is an independent design director working on brand identities, digital products and editorial websites.

For more than a decade I have helped founders, studios and product teams turn complex ideas into clear visual systems. Every project is different, but the goal is always the same: work that feels considered and is easy to use.

You can see some of that work in the selected projects or read about the process behind it in the journal.

How I work

Projects run in short, focused stages so decisions are shared and nothing is designed in isolation:

  • Discovery sessions to understand the brand, the audience and the goals.
  • Concept directions presented with real content, not placeholders.
  • Design and build handoff with a documented system your team can maintain.

Working together

I usually collaborate directly with a small core team and bring in trusted specialists for development, motion or photography when a project needs them.

Availability

New projects are booked a few months in advance. Brand identities typically take six to ten weeks, and websites between eight and twelve, depending on scope.
